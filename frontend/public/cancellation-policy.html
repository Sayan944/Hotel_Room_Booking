Policy

Cancellation & Refund Terms

These terms apply to all reservations made directly with LUXESTAY, unless a specific rate or promotion states different conditions at the time of booking.

No. 01

Standard Cancellation Window

  • Flexible-rate reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the scheduled arrival date.
  • Cancellations made within 48 hours of arrival are charged the equivalent of one night's stay plus applicable taxes.
  • The exact cut-off time and time zone for your reservation are shown in your booking confirmation email.
Cancel before arrival Charge
48 hours or more No charge — full refund
Less than 48 hours One night's stay + tax
No-show Full stay charged
No. 02

Non-Refundable Rates

  • Reservations booked under a "non-refundable" or "advance purchase" rate are paid in full at the time of booking and are not eligible for a refund upon cancellation.
  • Non-refundable bookings may still be eligible for a rebooking credit at the property's discretion, valid for 12 months, in cases of documented medical or family emergencies.
  • Rate type is always clearly labeled at checkout before payment is confirmed.
No. 03

Late Cancellations & No-Shows

  • A "no-show" occurs when a guest does not arrive by 6:00 AM the day after the scheduled check-in date without prior notice.
  • No-shows are charged the full first night's rate, and the remaining nights of the reservation are released.
  • Repeated no-shows on an account may result in a requirement for prepayment on future bookings.
No. 04

Early Departure

  • Guests who choose to check out earlier than their reserved departure date are not entitled to a refund for unused nights, unless arranged with the front desk prior to check-in.
  • Early departure fees, if any, will be disclosed at check-in for the specific rate booked.
No. 05

Group & Event Bookings

  • Bookings of 5 rooms or more, and event or wedding block bookings, follow a separate cancellation schedule outlined in the group contract.
  • Group cancellations typically require 30 days' notice to avoid a cancellation fee; exact terms are confirmed at the time of quotation.
No. 06

Peak Season & Holiday Bookings

  • Reservations spanning major holidays or peak local events may require a longer cancellation window, typically 7 to 14 days before arrival.
  • Any extended cancellation window applicable to your dates will be clearly stated before you confirm your booking.
No. 07

Modifying a Reservation

  • Date changes and room changes are treated as a new booking request and are subject to availability and any rate difference.
  • Modifications to flexible-rate bookings can be made free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival.
  • Non-refundable rates generally cannot be modified; contact the property directly to discuss options.
No. 08

Refund Processing

  • Eligible refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days of cancellation.
  • Processing times beyond that window are typically due to the guest's card issuer or bank, not LUXESTAY.
  • A confirmation email is sent once a refund has been initiated on our end.
No. 09

Exceptional Circumstances

  • In the event of natural disasters, government travel restrictions, or other circumstances beyond a guest's control, LUXESTAY may waive standard cancellation fees at its discretion.
  • Supporting documentation may be requested to process an exceptional cancellation.
No. 10

How to Cancel

  • Cancel online using the link in your booking confirmation email, through your LUXESTAY account, or by calling the property directly.
  • You will receive a cancellation confirmation number — please retain it for your records.
  • Cancellations made through a third-party booking site must be processed through that site's cancellation policy and channel.

Questions about a specific reservation? Contact the front desk or write to reservations@luxestay.example.
